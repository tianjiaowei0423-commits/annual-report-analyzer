# Credit summaries, 2025

Written by Claude from the ratio table in `output/ratios.xlsx`. The underlying figures were extracted from the annual reports and checked against a hand-made ground truth.

## DFDS

**DFDS has the highest leverage in the group and an EBIT that does not cover interest, though cash generation before investment is still positive.**

- **Profitability:** EBITDA margin is 12.1% and EBIT margin is 1.7%, both the lowest of the four. Net profit is DKK -425m, so the company made a loss for the year.
- **Debt:** Net debt / EBITDA is 4.1x on the common definition, which is high and the highest of the four; the company's own NIBD gives the same 4.1x. Interest cover is 0.7x, the lowest of the four, which means EBIT does not cover interest at all. The equity ratio is 36.2%, also the lowest.
- **Cash:** Cash conversion is 88.2%, second best, and capex intensity is 5.1%, the lowest of the four. FCF margin on the common definition is 5.6%, third of four. The company reports FCF of DKK 2,957m against DKK 1,724m on the common definition, because companies define free cash flow differently.
- **Watch:** Interest cover of 0.7x combined with 4.1x net debt / EBITDA: earnings need to improve for the debt load to be serviced from operating profit.

## Maersk

**Maersk has low leverage on the common definition, a high equity ratio and the best cash conversion of the four, with mid-table margins.**

- **Profitability:** EBITDA margin is 17.7% and EBIT margin is 6.5%, both second best. Net profit is USD 2,915m.
- **Debt:** The company reports net cash (NIBD USD -2,947m), while the common definition gives net debt of USD 9,878m, or 1.0x EBITDA. Companies define net debt differently, and leverage is low on the common view. Interest cover is 3.1x, third of four, and the equity ratio is 64.2%, second best.
- **Cash:** Cash conversion is 102.4%, the best of the four, so operating cash flow exceeds EBITDA. Capex intensity is 8.9% and FCF margin on the common definition is 9.2%, second best. Reported FCF is USD 2,232m against USD 4,962m on the common definition, because companies define free cash flow differently.
- **Watch:** Interest cover of 3.1x is the third best of the four and is the weakest point against low leverage and a high equity ratio.

## Norden

**Norden has the lowest leverage and good interest cover, but heavy investment pushes free cash flow negative on the common definition.**

- **Profitability:** EBITDA margin is 14.5% and EBIT margin is 5.1%, both third of four. Net profit is USD 120.3m.
- **Debt:** Net debt / EBITDA is 0.8x on both the company's definition and the common one, which is low and the lowest of the four. Interest cover is 4.3x, second best, and the equity ratio is 53.9%, third of four.
- **Cash:** Cash conversion is 83.2%, the lowest of the four. Capex (USD 630.1m) exceeds operating cash flow (USD 378.2m), and capex intensity is 20.2%, second highest. FCF is USD -251.9m on the common definition and the FCF margin is -8.1%, the lowest of the four; the company reports FCF of USD 170.4m, because companies define free cash flow differently.
- **Watch:** Capex above operating cash flow: free cash flow is negative on the common definition, so the low leverage of 0.8x depends on how long investment stays at this level.

## TORM

**TORM leads on margins, interest cover, equity ratio and FCF margin, with moderate leverage and the highest capex intensity.**

- **Profitability:** EBITDA margin is 42.6% and EBIT margin is 26.6%, both the best of the four. Net profit is USD 286m.
- **Debt:** Net debt / EBITDA is 1.5x on the common definition, which is moderate and third of four; the company's own NIBD gives the same 1.5x. Interest cover is 5.2x, the best of the four, and the equity ratio is 65.4%, also the best.
- **Cash:** Cash conversion is 87.4%, third of four. Capex intensity is 23.2%, the highest of the four, yet FCF margin on the common definition is 14.1%, also the best. Reported FCF is USD 346m against USD 188.6m on the common definition, because companies define free cash flow differently.
- **Watch:** Capex intensity of 23.2% is the highest in the group, so the FCF margin of 14.1% depends on earnings staying high while investment continues.

## Peer comparison

TORM leads on EBITDA margin (42.6%), EBIT margin (26.6%), interest cover (5.2x), equity ratio (65.4%) and FCF margin (14.1%), while Maersk has the best cash conversion at 102.4%. Norden has the lowest net debt / EBITDA at 0.8x, followed by Maersk at 1.0x and TORM at 1.5x. DFDS is last on EBITDA margin, EBIT margin, interest cover (0.7x) and equity ratio (36.2%), and is the only one with high leverage at 4.1x. Norden is last on FCF margin (-8.1%) because of heavy capex. Business models differ (ferries and logistics, container shipping, dry bulk and tankers, product tankers), so part of the gaps comes from the business and not only from how well each company is run.
