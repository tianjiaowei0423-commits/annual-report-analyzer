## DFDS

**DFDS has the highest leverage of the four at 4.1x net debt / EBITDA, and EBIT does not cover interest after interest cover fell from 1.8x to 0.7x.**

- **Profitability:** EBITDA margin of 12.1% and EBIT margin of 1.7% are both the lowest of the four companies. Net profit was DKK -425m.
- **Debt:** Net debt / EBITDA on the common definition is 4.1x, which is high; the company's own NIBD (DKK 15,260m) is close to the common-definition net debt (DKK 15,221m). Interest cover of 0.7x is below 1.0x, so EBIT does not cover interest at all. The equity ratio of 36.2% is the lowest of the four.
- **Cash:** Cash conversion of 88.2% is second best of the four, and capex intensity of 5.1% is the lowest. FCF margin on the common definition is 5.6%, third of four; the company reports FCF of DKK 2,957m against DKK 1,724m on the common definition, because companies define free cash flow differently.
- **Trend 2024→2025:** Interest cover fell from 1.8x to 0.7x and net debt / EBITDA rose from 3.9x to 4.1x. The EBIT margin fell from 5.1% to 1.7%.
- **Watch:** Interest cover below 1.0x combined with high leverage leaves little room if earnings weaken further.

## Maersk

**Maersk has low leverage (1.0x on the common definition) and a 64.2% equity ratio, but earnings weakened and interest cover fell from 6.5x to 3.1x.**

- **Profitability:** EBITDA margin of 17.7% and EBIT margin of 6.5% are both second best of the four. Net profit was USD 2,915m.
- **Debt:** The company reports net cash (NIBD of USD -2,947m), while the common definition gives net debt of USD 9,878m, or 1.0x EBITDA; companies define net debt differently, and leverage is low on either view. Interest cover of 3.1x is third of four, and the equity ratio of 64.2% is second best.
- **Cash:** Cash conversion of 102.4% is the best of the four. Capex (USD 4,799m) is well below operating cash flow (USD 9,761m), and the FCF margin of 9.2% is second best. Reported FCF of USD 2,232m differs from USD 4,962m on the common definition, because companies define free cash flow differently.
- **Trend 2024→2025:** Interest cover fell from 6.5x to 3.1x and the EBIT margin fell from 11.7% to 6.5%. Net debt / EBITDA rose from 0.8x to 1.0x.
- **Watch:** Whether margins and interest cover keep falling, since both have already dropped sharply.

## Norden

**Norden has the lowest leverage of the four at 0.8x, but capex of USD 630.1m exceeds operating cash flow of USD 378.2m and the FCF margin is -8.1%.**

- **Profitability:** EBITDA margin of 14.5% and EBIT margin of 5.1% are both third of four. The EBITDA margin improved from 12.7% to 14.5%, while the EBIT margin was flat at 5.0% to 5.1%.
- **Debt:** Net debt / EBITDA is 0.8x on both the company's definition and the common one, which is low and the best of the four. Interest cover of 4.3x is second best, and the equity ratio of 53.9% is third.
- **Cash:** Cash conversion of 83.2% is the lowest of the four. Capex intensity of 20.2% is second highest, and capex (USD 630.1m) exceeds operating cash flow (USD 378.2m), giving FCF of USD -251.9m and an FCF margin of -8.1%, the lowest of the four. The company reports FCF of USD 170.4m, because companies define free cash flow differently.
- **Trend 2024→2025:** Capex intensity rose from 11.7% to 20.2% and the FCF margin fell from -1.4% to -8.1%. Net debt / EBITDA rose from 0.5x to 0.8x.
- **Watch:** If capex stays above operating cash flow, leverage will keep rising from its current low level.

## TORM

**TORM leads on margins, interest cover and FCF margin, though margins fell from 2024 and net debt / EBITDA rose from 1.1x to 1.5x.**

- **Profitability:** EBITDA margin of 42.6% and EBIT margin of 26.6% are the best of the four. They fell from 54.6% and 42.3% in 2024.
- **Debt:** Net debt / EBITDA on the common definition is 1.5x, which is moderate and third of four; the company's own NIBD (USD 848m) is close to the common-definition net debt (USD 839.6m). Interest cover of 5.2x and an equity ratio of 65.4% are both the best of the four.
- **Cash:** Cash conversion of 87.4% is third of four, and capex intensity of 23.2% is the highest. The FCF margin of 14.1% is the best of the four; reported FCF of USD 346m differs from USD 188.6m on the common definition, because companies define free cash flow differently.
- **Trend 2024→2025:** Interest cover fell from 9.4x to 5.2x and net debt / EBITDA rose from 1.1x to 1.5x. The EBIT margin fell from 42.3% to 26.6%.
- **Watch:** The continued fall in margins and interest cover, since leverage has also risen.

## Peer comparison

TORM leads on EBITDA margin, EBIT margin, interest cover, equity ratio and FCF margin, while Norden has the lowest leverage at 0.8x and Maersk is next at 1.0x. DFDS lags on the profitability, leverage, interest cover and equity ratio measures, with net debt / EBITDA of 4.1x and interest cover of 0.7x. Norden has the lowest FCF margin at -8.1% and the lowest cash conversion at 83.2%, while Maersk has the best cash conversion at 102.4%. Business models differ (ferries and logistics, container shipping, dry bulk and tankers, product tankers), so part of the gaps comes from the business and not only from how well each company is run. Free cash flow and net debt are defined differently by each company, so the common-definition figures are the better basis for comparison.
